The Legend

This site is the digital evolution of Collin's famous Spreadsheet NHL Fantasy Playoffs — a handcrafted Excel masterpiece that has been the centrepiece of playoff season for years. Every pool, every pick, every argument about who got robbed in the draft: it all started there.

How the Draft Pools Work

All 16 NHL playoff teams' rosters are pulled at the start of the playoffs. Players are ranked by their regular season statistics and divided into draft pools so that every participant has access to players of similar calibre — no one can just pick the top 22 players.

Forwards (F1 – F12)

All playoff forwards (centres, left wings, right wings) are ranked by regular season points, with goals as the tiebreaker. The top 96 are pooled into 12 groups of 8:

Fantasy Points

Points are accumulated from playoff statistics only — regular season numbers don't count once the puck drops in Game 1.

Skaters (Forwards & Defencemen)
Goal+2 pts
Assist+1 pt

Formula: Goals × 2 + Assists

Goalies
Win+2 pts
Shutout win+4 pts

Formula: Wins × 2 + Shutouts × 2
A shutout win gives 4 pts total (2 for the win + 2 shutout bonus).

Rules
  • The draft closes when the admin starts the playoffs — no changes after that.
  • Each participant picks exactly one player from each pool (22 picks total).
  • Players from eliminated teams stop accumulating points and are shown with an ELIM badge.
  • Stats are refreshed nightly from the NHL API.
  • Tiebreakers are settled by goals (skaters) or wins (goalies).
  • The participant with the most fantasy points at the end of the Stanley Cup playoffs wins.
  • If there is a buy-in, the pot is split 60% / 30% / 10% across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
